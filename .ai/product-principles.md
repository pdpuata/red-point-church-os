# Product Principles

## Product model
**Church App** = member/public interface.
**Church OS** = operational system underneath.
**AI Operations Layer** = repeatable workforce operating on the OS.

The app is not the product. The reliable operational loop is the product.

## Opportunity filter
Score each process on:
- frequency
- pain
- labour hours
- error/consequence cost
- handoff count
- information loss
- AI automation potential
- strategic value

Then choose one of: eliminate, redesign, automate, augment, human-only.

## Core experience
The staff control tower should answer in under a minute:
- What is happening?
- What changed?
- What is at risk?
- What is overdue?
- What can AI handle now?
- What requires a human decision?
- What will break if we do nothing?
- Is Sunday ready?

## Design rule
If a user must open five screens to discover a problem the system already knows about, the product is unfinished.
