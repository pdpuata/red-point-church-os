## Short description

## Full description
Red Point Church puts the church's most important information in one simple place.

Use the app to:
- See what's happening next
- Find Sunday service information and directions
- Browse and search sermons
- Read current announcements
- Discover church ministries and leaders
- Let the church know you're coming
- Contact the church
- Receive useful church notifications when enabled

The app is designed to be simple and easy to use, including for visitors and people who are less comfortable with technology.
