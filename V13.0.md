# V13 — Church Experience Layer

The product now has two complementary identities:

1. **Public Church App** — visitors can discover Red Point, plan a Sunday, watch sermons, find events, explore church life and contact the church without signing in.
2. **My Red Point** — authenticated people can maintain a personal church connection profile, choose next-step interests and see relevant church-life opportunities.

The Staff/Admin Control Tower remains separate. AI and autonomous operations stay underneath these experiences and should progressively improve what members and visitors experience rather than becoming the visible product themselves.
