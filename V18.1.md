# V18.1 — AI Operating Phase 2

This release moves the Church OS from an AI-enabled collection of workflows toward an AI-operated institution.

## Live architecture

Organisational State → Event → Subscription → Workflow Dispatch → Autonomous Runtime → Deterministic Handler → Verification → Outcome → Audit → Learning.

## Phase 2 additions

- Event-driven workflow activation for service and roster changes.
- Autonomous setlist and Sunday readiness monitoring.
- Idempotent agent/workflow execution boundaries.
- People capability-gap detection that creates stable operational tasks.
- Elder-oriented readiness and attention surfaces.
- Deterministic AI executive attention brief RPC.
- Phase 2 operational health RPC.
- Internal OS RPC execution closed to anonymous/public callers.
- Explicit human approval boundaries remain for consequential people, communication and setlist decisions.

## AI operating principle

The system should increasingly move from:

Human opens screen → finds work → asks AI → performs work

to:

System observes → AI evaluates → safe action executes → system verifies → human receives only exceptions/decisions.

## Human authority invariant

AI may perceive, analyse, recommend, prepare and execute approved low-risk operational work. It may not silently change consequential people assignments, pastoral decisions, theology, governance policy or autonomy policy.

## Verification

CI must continue to pass Expo Doctor, release checks, TypeScript and the operational AI loop. Database health must be checked independently of UI claims.

## Known external prerequisites

Real device testing, notification tokens, visitor submissions, third-party credentials and licensed SongSelect access require the church's real environment and cannot be fabricated by the system.
